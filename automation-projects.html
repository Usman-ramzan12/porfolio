AI Website-Outreach
Build Gallery

Every site on this page was produced by my Claude-powered outreach automation. It finds local businesses on Google Maps that have no website, verifies a public business email, builds a one-page preview site from the real listing data (services, hours, Google reviews), runs automated QA, and hosts it in GoHighLevel, ready for outreach.

These are speculative preview builds made for outreach, not commissioned client work. Each one uses only the business's public listing data, and none of them are indexed by search engines.

Pipeline

What happens for every site

1. Find & qualify

Google Maps listings with no website, checked against the lead log and the CRM for duplicates.

2. Verify email

A public business email is confirmed (Facebook, directories) with a matching phone or owner name.

3. Build & QA

A unique one-page site from real data only, then an automated QA pass at 390px and 1440px.

4. Host & log

Published as a GoHighLevel funnel page, contact created with custom fields, lead log updated.
